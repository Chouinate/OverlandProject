01 — About

Made for dirt roads and long routes.

Pack Mountain Overland is a media + gear project documenting the rigs, routes, and rituals of overlanding the northeast. We build trucks that can sleep us, feed us, and get us home — then we point them at the next ridgeline.

Whether it's a weekend dispersed-camp run or a multi-week backcountry route, every trip becomes a story, a film, and a reason to refine the kit.
